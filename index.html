04

WHY IT MATTERS

Supporting better sourcing decisions

EMPLOYEE

Clearer decision support

TextileMap gives material sourcing employees quick access to supplier, material and sustainability data.

It simplifies comparisons and increases awareness of environmental impacts while supporting professional judgement rather than replacing it.

SOCIAL

Shared information

TextileMap creates a shared source of information that can improve collaboration and transparency between employees involved in sourcing decisions.

ENVIRONMENTAL & BUSINESS

More informed choices

By making sustainability data easier to compare, TextileMap can help users identify suppliers and materials with lower environmental impact.

This can support IKEA's wider sustainability goals while also improving the quality of sourcing decisions.

05

DESIGN THINKING

Usability, feasibility and viability

USABILITY

Designed for sourcing employees

TextileMap gives material sourcing employees quick access to supplier, material and sustainability information.

The dashboard is designed to simplify complex information and make comparisons easier while still supporting professional judgement.

FEASIBILITY

An internal software solution

TextileMap is designed as internal software for IKEA and could build on information already available within existing internal systems.

The main technical challenge is integrating data from different sources and ensuring that it is standardized, reliable and kept up to date.

VIABILITY

Supporting IKEA's goals

TextileMap supports sourcing decisions by making relevant sustainability and supply-chain information easier to understand and compare.

By improving access to existing information, the solution can support both more informed sourcing decisions and IKEA's wider sustainability and business goals.

06

DATA

Fair comparisons start with trusted data

Current approach

During development, TextileMap uses mock data to test the system, interface and comparison functionality.

Standardized metrics

By presenting important metrics such as carbon emissions and water usage consistently, TextileMap aims to make supplier comparisons clearer and easier to understand.

07

VALIDATION

How we explored the problem

User insight

To validate the idea, we interviewed a former sourcing manager and CEO with experience working with complex supply chains.

IKEA research

We also reviewed IKEA sustainability reports and strategy documents, including the sustainability strategy towards 2030.

These insights reinforced the challenge of managing complex supply chains and the value of providing sourcing employees with a clearer overview.

IKEA sustainability

One of the goals highlighted in IKEA's sustainability strategy is reducing greenhouse gas emissions.

TextileMap explores how sustainability information could become easier to use as part of everyday sourcing decisions.

08

SUMMARY

Bringing the information together

TextileMap helps sourcing managers make more informed decisions by bringing supply-chain and sustainability information together in one clear and accessible dashboard.

The concept is not about creating new data or replacing existing sourcing expertise.

It is about simplifying existing information and making it easier to understand and use.
