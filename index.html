ABOUT THE PROJECT

Why TextileMap?

The problem

IKEA has a large and complex textile supply chain involving many suppliers, materials and sustainability factors.

Relevant information is often spread across different reports and systems, making it difficult for sourcing managers to gain a clear overview and compare different options.

The solution

TextileMap is a dashboard that gathers information about textiles, suppliers and sustainability in one visual and structured interface.

The goal is to make information easier to find, easier to understand and easier to use when making sourcing decisions.

01

Supply-chain processes

Sustainability score

DESIGN THINKING & SUSTAINABILITY

Feasibility and sustainability

USABILITY

Designed for sourcing employees

TextileMap gives material sourcing employees quick access to supplier, material and sustainability information.

The dashboard is designed to simplify complex information and make comparisons easier while still supporting professional judgement.

FEASIBILITY

An internal software solution

TextileMap is designed as internal software for IKEA and could build on information already available within existing internal systems.

The main technical challenge is integrating data from different sources and ensuring that it is standardized, reliable and kept up to date.

VIABILITY

Supporting IKEA’s goals

TextileMap supports sourcing decisions by making relevant sustainability and supply-chain information easier to understand and compare.

By improving access to existing information, the solution can support both more informed sourcing decisions and IKEA’s wider sustainability and business goals.

EMPLOYEE

Better decision support

TextileMap empowers material sourcing employees by providing clear and quick access to supplier, material and sustainability data.

SOCIAL

Shared understanding

A shared source of information can improve collaboration and transparency between employees involved in sourcing decisions.

ENVIRONMENTAL

More sustainable choices

By making data such as emissions, water usage and renewable material content easier to compare, TextileMap can support lower-impact sourcing decisions.

DATA & TRUST

Fair comparisons need trusted data

TextileMap currently uses mock data, while the long-term value of the concept depends on reliable, standardized and transparent metrics.

VALIDATION

How we explored the problem

User insight

To validate the idea, we interviewed a former sourcing manager and CEO with experience working with complex supply chains.

These insights reinforced the challenge of managing complex supply chains and the need for a clearer overview.

IKEA research

We also reviewed IKEA sustainability reports and strategy documents, including the sustainability strategy towards 2030.

One of the goals highlighted in this strategy is the reduction of greenhouse gas emissions, which is relevant to the type of data TextileMap aims to visualize.
