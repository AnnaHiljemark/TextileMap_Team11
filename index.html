THE CHALLENGE

Supply-chain information can be difficult to compare.

Textile sourcing involves many suppliers, materials and sustainability factors. Relevant information may be spread across different reports, systems and data sources, making it difficult to build a clear overview before making a sourcing decision.

01

Scattered information

02

Complex supplier comparisons

03

Different sustainability indicators

OUR CONCEPT

TextileMap brings the supply chain into one interface.

The concept is a dashboard that visualizes supply chains for different textiles and suppliers. It is intended to provide overview, clarity and relevant details for decision-making.

CO₂ emissions Water usage Climate risk Supplier information Material comparison

“The goal is not to replace professional judgement — it is to make the information behind that judgement easier to understand.”

TextileMap design principle
02
DESIGN THINKING

Desirable. Feasible. Viable.

U
USER VALUE · USABILITY

Designed for material sourcing employees.

The primary users are employees working with material sourcing. TextileMap aims to help them access and compare relevant supply-chain and sustainability information without having to search through multiple disconnected sources.

  • Clear overview before deeper analysis
  • Support more informed sourcing decisions
  • Keep the user in control of the final judgement
F
TECHNICAL REALIZATION · FEASIBILITY

Built around trusted data and a simple interface.

The concept requires supplier sustainability data, emissions and water-use data, as well as geographical information that can support insights into climate risk.

  • Supplier and material datasets
  • Geographical and climate-related data
  • Integration with existing IKEA information
V
BUSINESS ALIGNMENT · VIABILITY

Better visibility can support better sourcing decisions.

TextileMap is intended to support IKEA’s work with responsible sourcing by making sustainability information easier to access, compare and use in everyday decision-making.

  • Supports sustainability-oriented sourcing
  • Creates a shared information view
  • Can evolve as data and business needs change

SUSTAINABLE SOFTWARE REQUIREMENTS

Looking beyond
the immediate interface.

We evaluate TextileMap across five sustainability dimensions and consider both positive and negative effects. The concept should support sustainable decisions without hiding uncertainty, reducing user agency or creating false confidence in incomplete data.

I

Individual

Easier access to information can increase user agency and learning. At the same time, over-reliance on the dashboard could reduce professional autonomy.

Agency Learning Privacy
S

Social

A shared information base could improve collaboration, but trust may be damaged if supplier data is misleading, incomplete or difficult to verify.

Trust Collaboration Transparency
E

Environmental

Better visibility of emissions, water usage and climate-related risks could support more sustainable sourcing choices, while the software itself still consumes energy and hardware resources.

CO₂ Water Resources
$

Economic

Better decision support can create business value through clearer sourcing choices, but development, integration, maintenance and data quality assurance all have costs.

Value Supply chain Maintenance
T

Technical

The system should remain usable, maintainable and adaptable as suppliers, materials, sustainability indicators and user needs change over time.

Scalability Maintainability Security

WHAT WE ARE WATCHING

Data quality is not a side issue.
It is central to the concept.

Reliable and comparable data is one of the biggest challenges. Inaccurate or misleading information could result in poor decisions and weaken trust in the system.

01

Data quality

Supplier and sustainability information may vary in quality, completeness and comparability.

02

False confidence

A polished dashboard can make uncertain information appear more reliable than it is.

03

Long-term relevance

Sustainability data, market conditions, regulations and supplier information change over time and require ongoing updates.
